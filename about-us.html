How we got here

Built out of frustration with the status quo.

Northgate Solar began with a simple belief — solar should be designed around the home, not the other way around.

We saw too many generic systems, unclear pricing, and installations built for speed rather than long-term performance. So we took a different approach.

Every project starts from scratch. We study the property, understand the energy needs, and build a system designed to perform for years to come.

No shortcuts. No one-size-fits-all solutions. Just better solar, built to last.
